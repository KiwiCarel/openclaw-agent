Privacy Policy

OpenClaw Agent. Last updated 30 October 2026.

Who uses this app

OpenClaw Agent is a private personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not available to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.

What data it accesses

With the owner's authorisation, the app may access the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts, only as needed to carry out tasks the owner requests.

How data is used

Data is used solely to perform tasks for the owner, such as finding files, summarising email or managing calendar events. To do this, relevant content may be processed by a server the owner controls and by the AI model provider the owner has chosen.

Sharing

Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with any third party except as needed to perform the owner's requested tasks as described above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
