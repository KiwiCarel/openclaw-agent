OpenClaw Agent

A private personal assistant

OpenClaw Agent is a personal AI assistant run by a single individual for their own use. It is not a public application, and it is not offered to, or usable by, anyone other than its owner.

The assistant helps its owner manage their own files, calendar and email through Google APIs, using an account the owner controls.

See the privacy policy for how data is handled.
